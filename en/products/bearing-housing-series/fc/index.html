Standard construction across all models: HT200 grey cast iron round flange housing with evenly spaced mounting holes, spherical inner cavity offering ±2° self-aligning capability to compensate shaft misalignment, anti-rust painted finish, designed to fit UC series insert bearings. Compact circular flange structure for space-saving surface mounting on equipment frames and machine walls.

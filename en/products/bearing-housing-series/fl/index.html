FAQ

Common questions

What bore sizes does the FL series cover?

The series follows the standard metric size steps: the last two digits of the model number give the shaft diameter, so FL208 fits a 28.6 mm shaft (1-1/8 in) and the sizes step up from there. Send us the shaft diameter and we will confirm the exact model.

What material is the FL housing made of?

Grey cast iron to HT200, the standard for this product family. The bearing seat is machined to an H7 tolerance band so the outer ring is held firmly without losing internal clearance.

Can the housing and bearing be supplied as a matched set?

Yes. FL is supplied as a complete unit with the insert bearing already fitted and the locking method set at the factory, so the housing and bearing cannot be mismatched on assembly.

How do I confirm FL is the right series for my machine?

Send the shaft diameter, the mounting surface (flat base, vertical plate or take-up frame) and a photo of the current part. We confirm the series, the bore and the locking type, then quote the matched set.
